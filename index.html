About This Portfolio

I am Josiah March, a student at Johns Hopkins University studying Applied Mathematics & Statistics and Economics.

This portfolio showcases three projects from my Technical Communications course, demonstrating how I adapt technical information for different audiences through professional writing, instructional design, and research-based reporting.

My Approach to Technical Communication

My background in applied mathematics, statistics, and economics has taught me the importance of communicating complex information clearly and accurately. I approach technical communication by considering the audience, identifying the most relevant information, and presenting it in a way that is accessible and useful.

Across these projects, I have practiced adapting my communication to different contexts, from presenting technical qualifications to employers, to teaching mathematical software to beginners, to explaining economic research to policymakers.
